# Painting Process – Three Steps

The lecture introduces three main painting-process stages:

## 1. Thinking

Ask:

- What is my concept?
- What am I trying to communicate?
- What images could represent the idea?
- What should appear in the painting?
- How should it be presented?

Thinking can include:

- Sketches
- Photographs
- References
- Collected materials
- Background ideas

---

## 2. Looking

Research and observe.

Look at:

- Nature
- People
- Objects
- Culture
- History
- Existing artworks
- Other artists

The **source** can stimulate an image or idea.

The **subject** becomes a tool for storytelling.

---

## 3. Doing

Begin working directly with the materials.

Doing involves:

- Experimentation
- Trial and error
- Intuition
- Applying techniques
- Making changes
- Reworking areas
- Developing the final painting

The important part is turning the idea into actual visual work.


# Final Portrait Process

1. Review the overall composition.
2. Check the face and head proportions.
3. Strengthen shading.
4. Develop lighter areas.
5. Develop darker areas.
6. Refine important facial features.
7. Keep the face as the major focal area.
8. Refine the background where needed.
9. Add details selectively.
10. Step back and evaluate the whole painting.
11. Make final corrections.
12. Photograph the completed work.

The complete process includes:

**Thinking → Looking → Doing**
