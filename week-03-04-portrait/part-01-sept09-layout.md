# Face and Head Construction

## Guidelines

Begin with:

- Vertical center line
- Horizontal center line

These guidelines should turn with the direction of the face.

## Basic Proportions

- Eyes sit slightly above the middle line.
- Face width is approximately five eye-widths.
- Nose width is approximately the space between the eyes.
- Cheekbones follow diagonal relationships.

Do not begin with tiny details such as eyelashes.

Start with the major structure first.

---

# Composition Planning

Before painting details, decide:

1. Placement of the portrait
2. Pose
3. Background
4. Shoulder position
5. Direction of the face
6. Major contour
7. Vertical and horizontal facial guidelines

- Bold contour lines

- Close-up composition
- Including a hand

The examples are ideas, not strict requirements.
